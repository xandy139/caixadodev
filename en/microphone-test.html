How In-Browser Microphone Testing & FFT Analysis Work

The Caixa do Dev Microphone Tester processes your audio input stream locally at 60 frames per second using the W3C Web Audio API and a 2048-point Fast Fourier Transform (AnalyserNode). You can inspect both frequency energy distribution and time-domain waveforms, measure decibels relative to full scale (dBFS), and record a 5-second sample in RAM to verify clarity.
