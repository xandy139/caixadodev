How In-Browser Webcam Testing Works

The Caixa do Dev Webcam Tester leverages the W3C WebRTC MediaDevices API to open a direct hardware video stream inside your browser. It inspects the decoded video frames to report your camera's true pixel dimensions, aspect ratio, and real-time frame rate (FPS) with zero external server communication.
